Teach it your spelling

The app spells the reversed sounds the way a text-to-speech engine reads them, which is not how you read them. This fixes that. Say a word, hear it backwards, and write down what you would have to read to make that sound.

Everything is stored in this browser as you go. Stop whenever — partial answers are still worth having, and the pieces are ordered so the early ones matter most.

Say this out loud and record it

Now — how would you spell what you just heard?

Write it so that if you read it cold, you would make exactly that sound. Real words are ideal. Nonsense is fine if that is genuinely what it sounds like.

How it goes

  • Space starts and stops recording. Enter saves and moves on.
  • Say the prompt word plainly, then it plays your own voice backwards. That backwards sound is the thing the app will ask you to say later — so spell it however you would actually read it back.
  • You are not being graded and there is no right answer. Your spelling is the right answer, because you are the one who has to read it.
