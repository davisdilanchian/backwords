Say this

Faded pieces are the closest sound English can spell — they land near the target, not on it.

Reading it

  • Every piece is spelled to be read the plain English way. ee as in knee, ah as in father, aw as in law, uh as in the.
  • Say the pieces evenly, with a small gap between them, and hold the vowels. Reversal eats anything you rush.
  • Flat and slow beats expressive. Pitch and stress come back inside out, so they only muddy it.

What it can't do

  • A word starting with h needs a breath at the end of a piece, and English has no letter for that. Exhale on the last vowel.
  • A word ending in ng needs a piece that starts with that sound, which English never does. It comes back closer to n.
